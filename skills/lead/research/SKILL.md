# Research

Read `skills/lead/README.md` first: sources are primary and dated, a forecast names its falsifier.

Hand the reading to a **background agent** when the host offers one, so the conversation goes on while it reads. Its job:

1. **Primary sources only** — the regulator's text, the vendor's own pages and price lists, the company's filings, the standard itself — not a write-up of them. Follow every claim back to the source that owns it.
2. **Each claim:** the source URL, its date (or *undated*, *mutable page*), the checked date, and whether it is documented or measured. A vendor's claim is the vendor's claim.
3. **Write the findings to one Markdown file**, each claim cited, where the lead repo keeps such notes; if it keeps none, say where you put it.
4. **End with what stays unknown** and what would change the answer.

Research informs a decision; it does not take one. The decision is the CEO's, as a role-tagged commit.
