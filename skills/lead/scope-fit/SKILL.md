# Scope fit

Read `skills/lead/README.md` first: the record comes first, a decision is a role-tagged commit.

The CEO loads this at idea intake. No product strategy document starts without a printed scope-fit table. The checks themselves are the instance's: its strategy baseline names them. The instance names its idea register and its strategy baseline in its lead AGENTS.md. This skill is the shape around them.

## Step 0: think the input through

A raw input (a spark, a note, a request) is not yet an idea. Before intake:

1. Read the strategy baseline.
2. Answer briefly on the baseline's own dimensions, and on two every instance has: **who owns it** (this company's product, or another team's or repo's) and **what already exists** (search the instance's idea register, the work list, the strategy and the decision log).
3. Write the candidates it opens, one line each, as many as are real, each marked *new* or *covered* with where.
4. The human picks. Each pick goes to its one home: an idea becomes a raw row in the idea register, an action a line on the work list, a question for another repo a request handed there. Nothing picked: the input is laid down. Nothing is written without the human's word.

An idea an agent brings is marked as the agent's and starts raw like any other.

## The table

| Check | Question | Pass / Fail | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| *a check of the baseline* | *its question* | Pass | *why, with where the record shows it* |

- One row per check of the baseline. Pass or fail, never *partly*; the reason cites the baseline or the record.
- One row stands in every instance: *Who could be harmed if this works exactly as designed? Who if it fails?*
- A fact the table needs and the record lacks: `research` first, then the row.

## The verdict

| Verdict | Means | Logged in the idea register |
|---|---|---|
| **Go** | The idea fits | Go; the product strategy skeleton starts |
| **Pivot** | Valid, but it belongs elsewhere | *pivoted to* the place, with the reason; no strategy document |
| **No-Go** | A check fails | *rejected*, with the failed check and the reason; the chain stops |

The verdict is the human's (`CORE.md` §4); the CEO writes it as a role-tagged commit. Nothing checks yet that every product strategy document has its Go in the idea register; the instance's lead gate goal could, by a search.

## Not here

- The counter-case against a Go before it is committed: `challenge`.
- The content of the strategy document, and the checks themselves: the instance's lead repo.
