# BOOKSHELF PS

A sample problem statement. It is made up and describes no real product.

## 1. Problem

A reader owns more books than they remember. A list in a notes app loses the order and never says what has been read.

## 2. Persona and job to be done

**The reader.** About a hundred books, read in bursts, mostly on a phone, often one-handed.

JTBD: *When I finish a book, I want to mark it read, so that the shelf shows what is left.*

## 3. Success

- A reader adds a book in under ten seconds. This is a hypothesis for the field, not a test: readers measure it, CI does not.
