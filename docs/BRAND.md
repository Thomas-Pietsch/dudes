# Brand

> The identity on one page. These decisions are made. To change one, change it here first, with the human's word, then everywhere it shows.

## The package: the seal

| | Decision |
|---|---|
| Package | **The seal** |
| Category | Local, spec-first AI software delivery with a human sign-off, recorded in git. Retired: "open-source company engine" and "ADE". Readers outside the project did not understand them |
| The dudes | The workers, as a plural figure of speech. Non-human, faceless, not characters: nothing remembers a conversation. They appear once, lightly, and never in the hero |
| The human | Neutral. Holds the stamp |
| Tagline | **Agents deliver. You seal.** |
| Humor line | *The dudes are chill. The gates are not.* Placed next to a gate refusal, where it is proven (the README tour), never at the top |
| Mnemonic | **D-U-D-E-S** = Define, Unit-test, Develop, Evaluate, Seal. Used in "How it works" in the README, never as the headline |
| The wink | 🦭 (yes, that kind of seal too). A wink in text, never a mascot |
| Sign-off | **Stay cool. Ship sealed.** |

Written DUDES in prose, `dudes` in the wordmark and on the command line.

## Voice

**Stay cool. Be direct. Flow with it. Humor is allowed, never at the expense of clarity.**

- **Stay cool.** State what you did, move on.
- **Be direct.** No filler. Say the thing, then stop.
- **Plain words first.** A stranger reads the first screen. Nothing on it that the page does not define.
- **Flow with it.** Something breaks? Adapt, fix, continue.
- **Humor sits next to a hard fact and never replaces it.** *The dudes are chill. The gates are not.* stands beside a real refusal.
- **Say what was measured.** Publish the failures too.

Yes: "Gate failed: the tests pass before the code exists, so they test nothing. Rewrite them."
No: "Oops! Something went wrong."

Yes: "Local, spec-first AI software delivery with a human sign-off, recorded in git."
No: "An open-source company engine."

## Never show or say

- **Real people.** No face, name or likeness of a real person: no actors, founders, users or stock-photo people.
- **Anything from a film.** No quotes, no characters, no props, no settings. The dudes are workers, not a person, and no single figure stands for them.
- **Lookalikes.** No mascot or logo that resembles another project's. Search before adding art. The animal seal stays a wink in text; other projects own that ground.
- **Faces on the dudes.** They have none. No human faces anywhere in the identity.
- **Vermilion outside the seal.**

## Brief: an illustration of the dudes

Not made yet. When it is, it must be this:

- **Scene.** A pipeline board, left to right: Define, Unit-test, Develop, Evaluate, Seal. Work items move along it as cards, one commit each.
- **The dudes.** A handful of role robots at the board, one per kind of step: spec, tests, code, QA, docs. Told apart by silhouette and tool, not by face. They stand for kinds of step, not for persistent characters. Faceless: a smooth head or a plain visor, no eyes, no mouth, nothing human. Calm and busy. Not cute, not menacing.
- **The human.** Not shown. Only the stamp appears, poised over the last column. It is the one vermilion thing in the picture.
- **Style.** Flat vector. Ink navy on paper, thin lines, monospace labels. No gradients, no photorealism. No text beyond the column labels and the tagline.
- **Check before use.** No human face. No lookalike of another project's mascot or logo. Nothing from the never-list.
